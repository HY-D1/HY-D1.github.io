# Portfolio project instructions

## Language

Use English for all portfolio project artifacts, including website copy,
documentation, code comments, commit messages, pull request titles and
descriptions, issue content, and verification or progress records.

Conversation with the owner may remain in Chinese unless English is requested.

## Content accuracy

Use only owner-provided or verified education, work, project, and contact details.
Do not invent accomplishments or fill missing facts with plausible assumptions.

## Visual direction

Keep the portfolio minimal, professional, and content-led. Use black, white,
and neutral gray with restrained typography, whitespace, and thin dividers.
Avoid decorative gradients, animated backgrounds, generic card grids, and
unsupported marketing claims. Preserve readable contrast and keyboard access.

Short, subtle entrance and spring-like interaction feedback are welcome. Keep
motion bounded, avoid loops or parallax, and disable nonessential motion for
reduced-motion preferences. Personal photographs must be owner-provided; never
substitute stock or generated images. Remove sensitive metadata from web copies,
use accurate alt text, and do not publish precise home or travel coordinates.
Keep photographs in compact three-column thumbnail rows, not large banners.
Preserve the entire composition in thumbnails, allow full-image viewing, and
retain the random three-photo selection with a usable no-JavaScript fallback.

## Maintenance

Keep changes small and preserve the dependency-free static site where practical.
Inspect the affected files and verify the relevant behavior before claiming success.
