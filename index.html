03 Working principles

Good work starts before the code.

Three habits for turning an open question into something people can use.

  1. 01

    Start with the question

    Make the problem specific enough to learn from and useful enough to matter.

  2. 02

    Make structure visible

    Use clear models, readable interfaces, and decisions that can be explained.

  3. 03

    Leave room to refine

    Treat the first version as a measured step, not the final answer.
